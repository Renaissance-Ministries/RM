### 3. California Manufacturing Is a Problem

- Victory Circle would likely not pass as a medical device manufacturer

**States with NO separate medical device manufacturing license required:** Utah, Minnesota, Michigan, Indiana, North Carolina, Virginia, Wisconsin.

### 5. DME Supplier Enrollment — Deep Dive
The group ran a detailed cost analysis on becoming their own DME supplier:

**Facility requirements (CMS):**
- 200 sq ft minimum practice location
- Public access (not gated/restricted)
- Staffed 30 hours/week minimum
- Permanent signage, posted hours
- Landline telephone (no cell as primary)
- Must permit unannounced CMS inspections
- Cannot share location with another Medicare supplier
- Closed-door (no retail showroom) is explicitly allowed
- Warehouses don't need separate enrollment

**California-specific:** If selling Rx devices, must have a licensed exemptee or pharmacist in charge on premises. Other states: anyone can staff the desk.

**Costs:**
- One-time setup: $9,500–$25,000 (application fees, accreditation, surety bond)
- Annual ongoing: $50,000–$78,000 (staff, rent, compliance, accreditation renewal)
- Accreditation takes 6–9 months
- CMS enrollment processing: 3–6+ months
- Total timeline: 12–18 months before first billable claim
- Break-even: ~30 units/year sustained

**Moratorium update:** A nationwide DME enrollment moratorium ran Feb 27 – Aug 27, 2026. It has expired. Enrollment is open again, but CMS can reimpose in 6-month increments.

### 6. DME Acquisition Option
Two types of listings found on dmelisting.com:
- **Shell companies** ($175K): Brand new, clean PTAN, zero billing history. BUT: under 36 months old, so majority ownership change triggers fresh enrollment — defeats the purpose.
- **Operating businesses** ($300K): e.g., Van Nuys, LA — 15 years operating, licensed, accredited, Medicare + Medi-Cal numbers, 40+ HMO/PPO contracts (some closed to new suppliers).

**Key insight:** The acquisition target should be an OLD, boring, long-enrolled operator (36+ months, no ownership changes), not a new clean shell. Asset purchases don't transfer enrollment — must be equity deal.

### 7. Recommendation from Analysis
**Do not build or buy a DME operation now.**
- Not on cost grounds (manageable) — on timing grounds (12–18 months before first claim)
- Current volume is near zero; break-even is 30 units/year
- 4 other channels need no code: institutions, schools, facilities, cash
- Best path: find one enrolled DME dealer willing to carry the product, test the channel, then revisit self-enrollment at 30+ units sustained
