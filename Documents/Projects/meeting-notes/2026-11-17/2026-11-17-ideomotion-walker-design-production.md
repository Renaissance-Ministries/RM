## Engineering Notes

- **Elastic vs. inelastic deformation:** If the walker springs back after load removal, it's operating in the safe zone (elastic). If it stays bent, it's failed (inelastic). One successful 500 lb test is sufficient evidence for now — full MTBF (mean time between failure) testing (1,000–100,000 cycles) is impractical at this stage.
- **Cable pre-tension problem:** Pre-stretching cables during assembly would require turnbuckles, adding assembly complexity — conflicts with the goal of easy modular assembly.
- **Switch wire as failure mode:** Any external movable wire is a "string that wants to be pulled" — design for easy replacement of the switch cable as a wear part.
- **Competitor benchmark:** Rifton offers 250 lb and 350 lb rated models. Must match 350 lb minimum to compete.
