## Editor's Notes (Corrections)

1. John Bonham died September 1980, not 1984 (alcohol-related asphyxiation). Led Zeppelin disbanded afterward.
2. The Eagles broke up in 1980 and reunited in 1994 (Hell Freezes Over). Don Felder fired in 2001. Glenn Frey died in 2016.
3. Sgt. Pepper released 1967; White Album 1968. Beatles broke up 1970.
4. The "salt and pepper" story: Paul McCartney misheard roadie Mal Evans. The accent is Scouse (Liverpool), not Cockney.
5. Journey's karaoke-discovered singer is Arnel Pineda (joined 2007); documentary is Don't Stop Believin': Everyman's Journey (2012).
6. Monkees spelling is Monkees; Cream track is "Tales of Brave Ulysses."
