## 4. Agency, Will, and Moral Ecology: Graded Volition in a Relational System


Having established the emergence of macro-consciousness from CP mechanics, this section explores agency and will as extensions of gradient-driven processes, framed within a moral ecology that modulates the “I.” In CPP, agency is not absolute but graded, evolving from reactive proto-decisions to mature pivots accessible via the “I.” Will serves as an optimization tool, stimulated by dissatisfaction and receding in harmony. Moral choices (good/evil) feed back into neural cohort stability and coherence via external/social loops, resulting in fragmentation and distraction from the pull of multiple persistent stressors that are irrelevant to attentional presence and joy in the Moment. This relational system underscores teleology: Choices serve the divine experience of the human experience, with evil fragmenting agency and presence and good enhancing entityhood.


### Spectrum of Agency: From Reactive to Mature Pivots


Agency manifests on a spectrum, emergent from cohort dynamics and graded by maturation. At the proto-level, CPs exhibit reactive volition—biased by gradients and deterministic at base (habits as myelinated paths). Macro-agency arises with the “I”: Triangulated cohorts enable meta-perspective (self/consequences/others), allowing pivots from reactive loops. Determinism influences (e.g., entrenched habits), but free will is axiomatic for practicality—access to a divine “well” of uncaused choice, transcending causal chains via CP awareness and defying regressive proof like existence itself. This contrasts with compatibilist views, where will is redefined as uncoerced deterministic action (e.g., Humean influences without contradiction to causality), by positing true uncaused pivots beyond mechanistic determinism.


Developmentally, agency starts low (infant reactivity) and rises with nurture (introspection/empathy), modulated by health/trauma/toxins. In CPP, this grades cohort coherence: Dense sync yields higher agency (e.g., moral deliberation); fragmentation reduces it (e.g., entrenched habits).


### Will as Optimization Tool: Stimulation and Recession


Will emerges as a tool for gradient optimization, stimulated by dissatisfaction (pain/emptiness as negative SSS differentials). It drives evaluation—assigning valences (approach/avoid)—and pivots (changing roads). In harmony, will recedes: Unself-conscious bliss (minimal “I”) maximizes relational joy, per teleology—serving God’s experience via aligned choices.


Teleologically, will enables co-creation: Pivots toward godly standards (love/peace) optimize divine canvas, joy stays low when ignored. From a secular perspective, this optimization could alternatively align with evolutionary adaptations for social cohesion or psychological well-being, enhancing survival and fulfillment without invoking divinity.


### Good vs. Evil Dynamics: Cumulative Feedback and Ecology


Moral ecology shapes agency via feedback: Isolated acts of unintentional good/evil engage self-other triangulation without inherent short term personal unification/decoherence—both stimulate gradients (pleasure from satisfaction of desire). Purposeful evil may originate from boredom, injustice, curiosity, or rebellion, resulting in a commitment of will to act against life, its sustaining rules, or their source. The intended goal of such intentional antagonism may the glee of destruction, inflicted pain, dominance, or oblivion. Focusing personal identity on the destruction of the created order diminishes the group identity. The perpetrator eventually suffers the same fate as the diminished environment ceases its support of survival. Evil, in its full spectrum of magnitude, ensnarls in procedural entanglements (unresolved consequences), habituated loops (repetitive desire), fragmented attention, and scattered cohorts. Good minimizes overhead: Quick resolutions via standards (e.g., the Ten Commandments as lookup tables) foster pride/presence and enhance external/societal harmony, allowing time/space for focus, personal coherence, and the joy of being fully in the Moment. In a pleasure-seeking system, evil’s unfairness breeds avoidance/anesthesia, reducing agency; good enables full-duplex harmony.


### Victim-Perpetrator Feedback: Fragmentation and Accountability


Evil received fragments via justice-clamor (avoidance loops); committed via consequence-management—both erode agency—but the spectrum differentiates: victims’ imposed fragmentation vs. perpetrators’ self-chosen. Systemic evil (e.g., societal unfairness denying merit) amplifies victim loops, entrenching avoidance beyond individual acts—highlighting perpetrators’ agency in perpetuating cycles. Accountability scales: Aversion for partial-agency individuals, counsel/confinement for diminished/defiant. Forgiveness resets: Dissolving entanglements, countering animal emotions to restore coherence. In CPP terms, forgiveness acts as a gradient-reset mechanism, dissolving PSR loops (persistent noise from unresolved SSS disruptions) and reducing cohort variance—enabling SSS realignment and linking back to earlier discussions on cohort synchronization.

## 5. Testability and Empirical Corroboration


While CPP’s Planck-scale foundations elude direct observation, macro-proxies enable testability, corroborating the model through neuroscience data. ### Proxies for CP Behavior: Macro-Signatures of Micro-Mechanics


Gamma oscillations (30-100 Hz) serve as proxies for DI chaining and cohort binding, reflecting synchronized gradients in PFC during self-reflection and moral tasks. Phase sync (PSRs superimposition analogs) binds features, interpreted as SSS reinforcement at neural scales. However, gamma activity also occurs in non-conscious states (e.g., during anesthesia or deep sleep, though at reduced amplitudes and without the task-specific synchronization seen in awake awareness), suggesting it is necessary but not sufficient for consciousness—consistent with CPP’s graded emergence, where full “I” requires additional cohort triangulation.


### Existing Data: Correlates and Disruptions


PFC gamma correlates with awareness: Neuronal discharges follow perceptual consciousness, with gamma explicit in visual tasks. Post-stimulus ~200-300 ms, PFC activity predicts unified percepts. Disruptions (TMS/psychedelics) fragment “I”: TMS to PFC impairs metacognition, reducing consciousness in disorders. Chronic stress (evil consequences) reduces PFC coherence: Impairs cortico-limbic sync, weakening function. Forgiveness practices boost it: Meditation elevates alpha/gamma, clearing rumination for coherence.


Recent 2025 studies further strengthen these proxies. For instance, prefrontal gamma oscillations engage dynamic cell type-specific configurations to support flexible behavior, with cross-hemispheric parvalbumin interneuron (PVI) gamma synchrony playing a central role in cognitive flexibility (Phensy et al., 2025, DOI: 10.1101/2024.03.08.584173v3). This aligns with CPP’s prediction of gradient-driven pivots under moral ambiguity, where synchronized cohorts enable agency shifts. Additionally, parietal gamma oscillations are decreased in the cognitive biotype of depression, indicating disrupted binding in parietal regions associated with cognitive processing (Zhao et al., 2025, DOI: 10.1038/s41598-025-20977-9). Such decreases corroborate evil’s fragmenting effects (e.g., chronic stress as persistent noise), reducing coherence in moral pivots and addressing gaps in how trauma impacts agency spectra.


### Proposed Experiments: Testing Predictions


TMS on PFC during dilemmas: Apply repetitive TMS (rTMS) at 1 Hz for 10 minutes to the dorsolateral PFC (DLPFC) to disrupt local synchrony, then measure fMRI gradient maps and EEG coherence for “I” fragmentation/agency spectra (e.g., reduced gamma sync during moral choice tasks like trolley problems). EEG in meditation (godly harmony) vs. trauma (evil fragmentation): Use high-density EEG to compare pre/post 20-minute forgiveness-focused meditation sessions (e.g., guided imagery) in PTSD patients vs. controls, quantifying alpha/gamma coherence changes in PFC/hippocampal regions.


### Gaps and Limits: Scale Challenges and Proxies


Planck untestable directly (quantum gravity needed); focus on proxies like microtubule coherence (Orch-OR). Simulations bridge: Cohort models with gradient-chasing test thresholds under moral perturbations. For falsifiability, if PFC gamma sync persists in fully aware states without correlating to reported “I” experiences (e.g., via real-time neuroimaging in lucid dreaming without expected SSS proxies), or if no coherence differences emerge in moral vs. neutral tasks, the model would be invalidated—highlighting reliance on indirect measures.

import numpy as np
import matplotlib.pyplot as plt

# Simple cohort sim: Gradient-chasing CPs under noise
def simulate_cohort(steps=50, num_cps=5, noise_level=0.5, godly_bias=0.2):
    coherence = np.zeros(steps)
    maturity = 0  # Tracks accumulated positive signals, threshold 0.7 for bias activation
    polarization = 0  # Cumulative mean signal, simulating alignment
    for t in range(steps):
        signals = np.random.uniform(-1, 1, num_cps) + (godly_bias if maturity > 0.7 else 0)
        signals += np.random.normal(0, noise_level, num_cps)
        coherence[t] = np.mean(np.abs(signals))
        maturity += 0.05 if np.mean(signals) > 0 else 0.02  # Faster growth for positive means
        polarization += np.mean(signals) * 0.01
        if coherence[t] < 0.2:
            break
    return coherence
