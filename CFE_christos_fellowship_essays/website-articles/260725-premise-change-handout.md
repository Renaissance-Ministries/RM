# 260725 - Premise Change Handout

# **Why Was a Savior Necessary?**


### The Argument in One Page


CHRISTOS FELLOWSHIP  ·  SUNDAY DISCUSSION  25 July 2026


**Reading rule: **The *fact* of the atonement is settled (Register 1). Only the *mechanism* is under discussion (Register 3). If every premise below were wrong, the cross would stand unaffected.


### **The Premises**


|
**P1** |
God is self-existent. There is nothing outside Him. *(Ex. 3:14; Acts 17:28)* |
 |

|
**P2** |
God is light; in Him is no darkness at all. *(1 John 1:5)* |
 |

|
**P3** |
His purity is an ongoing *separation* of light from dark — so admitting darkness is not distasteful to Him but self-annihilating. |
 |

|
**P4** |
God is love. *(1 John 4:8)* |
 |

|
**P5** |
Love cannot be satisfied alone, nor by a compelled response. |
 |

|
**P6** |
∴ Free others must exist, capable of genuine refusal. |
 |

|
**P7** |
Genuine refusal requires real access to the alternative — proximity to evil. |
 |

|
**P8** |
Moral disorder is *conservative*: it does not fade, and later good cannot reach back to cancel earlier wrong. |
 |


### **The Contradiction**


|
Free creatures **must** exist (P4–P6).   ·   They **must** be near evil (P7).


God **cannot** abide evil in His presence (P2–P3).   ·   There is **nowhere outside God** to put them (P1).


**These four cannot all be satisfied — unless a region can be sealed off *within* God.** |
 |


**The Resolution**


|
**P9** |
God demonstrably *can* partition — the rebel angels are sequestered, not annihilated. **Continued existence, terminated fellowship.*** (2 Pet. 2:4; Jude 6)* |
 |

|
**P10** |
The partition is the creation, made and held by **the Son** — who can look upon evil without expelling it. *(John 1:3)* This is why the Son, not the Father, is Creator. |
 |

|
**P11** |
Because the domain’s *exit* opens onto the Father, its law must be as exact as His. Hence sin genuinely incurs death; hence *eye for eye* — **exactness**, not vengeance. |
 |

|
**P12** |
∴ No creature can pay: his good works arrive **too late** (P8), and paying in full costs him the existence the payment was meant to preserve. |
 |


### **The Chain to the Cross**


Remedy must be generated **inside** the domain, by one **innocent** and **sovereign** within it


**→  **the Creator incarnates; a real body, killable, carrying no sentence


**→  **dies **unjustly** — in an exact ledger every sin demands a death, so every death demands a sin; His is a **credit with no matching debt**


**→  ****rises**, because the sentence found nothing in Him to grip — a treasury requires a living treasurer *(Heb. 7:25)*


**→  ****administers** it as gatekeeper: His domain, His law, His payment, His threshold *(John 14:6)*


**→  ****applied on reception** — because imposing it would destroy at the last step the very freedom the whole creation was built to protect.


### **Two distinctions that keep this from collapsing:**


- He bore our **liability**, not our **disharmony** — *laid on* him, a burden held, not a stain caught *(Isa. 53:6)*. The wrath gripped the sin, never the Man.

- The cross **generated** the answer to every sentence; it did not **execute** every sentence. A pattern applied, not a stock drawn down — so it never runs out, and cancels nothing until brought into phase with it.


### **The Conclusion**


**The Savior is not an accommodation. He is a structural necessity** — the only bridge between a holiness that cannot tolerate darkness and a love that requires free creatures who can choose it. ***At-one-ment***: what sin partitioned (Isa. 59:2), alignment with the Son rejoins (Eph. 2:13).


### **Pick Your Fight — Where to Push**


*Choose one. Naming the premise you doubt is more useful than doubting the whole.*


- **P3** — Is God’s purity really *constituted* as separation? Does the argument survive without it?

- **P7** — Could God have made creatures free to choose only among goods? Must freedom include access to evil?

- **P8** — Why can’t grace simply *heal* disorder? Is disorder truly conserved, or is that a physics metaphor overreaching?

- **P11** — Could the Son have run a lenient world and cleaned everyone at the gate? Why must the inside be as severe as the outside?

- **Reception** — If the remedy is sufficient for all, why not apply it to all? Is the answer freedom — or is it arbitrary?


*“For if, when we were enemies, we were reconciled to God by the death of his Son, much more, being reconciled, we shall be saved by his life.”  **—  Romans 5:10*
