## Isak's Photon/Bush Observation

- Isak described watching light reflect off a bush and tracing the experience:
  - Photons from the sun → redistributed by objects (not losing energy) → interpreted by eyes at different angles
  - "I'm not looking at a bush. I'm looking *towards* a bush. The only reason I'm seeing it is because something else is showing me it."
  - Connected to dual slit experiment: stream of photons → wave pattern; single photon → point
  - Extended to CPP: if we're all receiving something from a source, it's not just photons from the sun — it's coming from every direction at once
  - Clusters of "decided particles" that behave together, form DNA, follow rules, repeat as they die and are reborn
  - "It's quite like the sun — we take it for granted... actually quite invisible to us until we trust that it's there"

## Conscious Entity Theory

- More gestalt view: any pattern with sufficient "critical mass of consciousness" creates its own identity — a **conscious entity**
- Any concentration of consciousness has beingness; with space around it, you get distinction
- A rock is just an "it" until someone frames it: doorstop vs. museum fossil — same object, different identity
- **Identity depends on what the observer brings** — if you bring nothing, you won't even notice it
- This is the origin of Thomas's conscious entity concept: anything can be made into a conscious entity with a beingness associated with its concentratedness
