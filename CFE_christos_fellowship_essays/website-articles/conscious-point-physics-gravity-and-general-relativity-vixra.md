### 2.1 Conscious Points (CPs)


CPs are the indivisible, Planck-scale units of existence in CPP, each carrying exactly one elementary charge (\pm 1e) and one bit of internal state. In the context of gravity, CPs form the substrate from which spacetime emerges. ### 2.2 Displacement-Increment (DI) Bits

Relevant to gravity, DI-bit flow creates currents analogous to diffusion: \mathbf{J} = -D \nabla \phi, where D = c \ell_{\text{Pl}} / 2 is the diffusion constant. Bit conservation (\partial \phi / \partial t + \nabla \cdot \mathbf{J} = 0) yields the diffusion equation, which in the non-relativistic limit recovers Poisson’s equation for Newtonian gravity.
