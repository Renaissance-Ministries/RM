Additionally, in one of our guidances, it explains what they call subsequent product codes — where something has more than one function, it also may have more than one product code. **Isak:** How do we go about figuring that out? We were wondering if there were regulatory things we would need to follow to be compliant. **FDA Agent:** Well, if it has the lift capability, the agency would still consider it to be a lift.

**Isak:** So you're saying that if it lifts at all, then it needs to fall under that classification?

**FDA Agent:** Yeah, they would probably consider it to be a lift if it does the lifting.

**Isak:** What if the primary function is for therapeutic use and for walking, and the lift is just a function to get the user to operate it — does that affect it at all?

### 513(g) Process

**FDA Agent:** There is a process called 513(g). The FDA gives a suggested classification and governing regulation for your device.

- **FY2026 standard fee:** $7,820
- **FY2027 standard fee:** $8,596
- **FY2027 small business fee:** $4,298
- One-time fee per submission (not yearly)

**Small business request timeline:**
- Submit small business qualification request now
- Determinations sent out in October (fiscal year starts October)
- Deadline for small business requests: approximately July 31, 2027
- First in, first out review basis

**Isak:** Is this required?

**FDA Agent:** No, it's just for convenience — to have the FDA give a suggested classification.

---

### Risk of Wrong Classification

**Isak:** What are the ramifications if we accidentally picked the wrong classification?

**FDA Agent:** If you pick a product code that doesn't require any sort of pre-market authorization, and it turns out to be one that does, the agency could make you stop selling the device until you've submitted the pre-market submission and the FDA has authorized it for marketing. So it could mean a hiatus between when you're selling it and when you could sell it again.

**Isak:** Is the lift category one that requires pre-market authorization?

**FDA Agent:** It depends which product code. The electric one — FNG, "Lift, patient, AC-powered" — is Class II, but it says 510(k) exempt. So you don't have to have pre-market authorization. You can find the governing regulation and submission type in the product classification database for that particular product code.
