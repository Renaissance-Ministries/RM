CPs are displaceable, polarity-carrying (\pm) entities: eCPs for electromagnetic interactions (leptons/photons) and qCPs for strong force (quarks/hadrons). - **Aggregation and Emergence:** CPs chain via overlapping PSRs (\ell_p, as c-velocity isotropy enables omnidirectional bit propagation for relational diversity), forming DPs (pairs), tetra-cores (protons/neutrons), and cages (generations). ### 2.2 Grid Points (GPs): The Immobile Metric and Feedback Lattice


GPs are fixed, non-charge CPs (~10^{30} per Planck length) forming space’s discrete scaffold. They integrate incoming DI bits into SSS fields, providing CPs with relational data.


- **Role in Motion/Change:** GPs enable distance/motion by summing bits; without them, no stable gradients or environmental analysis. SSS at GPs biases CP choices, closing the feedback loop for “lawful” behavior.
