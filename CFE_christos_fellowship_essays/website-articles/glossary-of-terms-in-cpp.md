# Glossary of Terms In CPP

Glossary of Key Terms and Acronyms

Conscious Point (CP)  The fundamental entity of awareness of self and others in Conscious Point Physics (CPP): CPs are an irreducible point of awareness which follow the ruleset for their type (q or e) and polarity (+/-). The CPs are aware of their environment, in particular, the SSV (Space Stress Vector) summation at their Grid Point (GP). CPs are the basic “bits” of physical reality. As unpaired CPs (i.e., not bound in a DP/Dipole Particle), they attract the opposite polarity pole of the local DPs and create a polarized DP cloud (as in an unpaired electron, creating .511 MeV of mass energy/DP Sea polarization). The CPs must choose either to obey their ruleset or to obey an override command; thus, they engage in proto-volition. Their awareness of the local environment, when organized and coordinated, enables the emergence of macro-level consciousness.

\item[Displacement Increment (DI) Bit] The unit of instruction Displacement instructions sent from GP to GP at one PSR (which is dependent on the local SSV). The DI-Bit is the smallest motion/Displacement Increment, and does not necessarily correspond to one GP increment (10^30 GPs per PSR in unstressed space). In CPP, the DI-bit is a binary-coded signal stream propagated between GPs along 600-cell lattice pathways. The PSR (Planck Sphere Radius) is the distance light travels in one Moment/clock tick and depends on the SSV, reproducing the Lorentz factor, gamma, and relativistic effects via a 600-cell expansion. DI-bits carry information: the originating position, the SSV information from distant and local (within PSR) sources. The DI bits inform the local GPs at PSR distance of the presence of a CP on that GP. The GPs sum the DI-bit information from local GPs/CPs, distant CPs, to compute their local Space Stress Vector (SSV) and PSR. The GPs receive and emit the same number of DI-bits. Variation in PSR means that DI-bit flow varies with SSV, resulting in a dynamic DI-bit flow rate. The Nexus/source/governing mind ensures that DI-bits are conserved.

Dipole Particle (DP) The family of Dipole Particles includes the qDPs (plus/minus quark-type qCPs), eDPs (plus/minus electron-type eCPs), and hDPs (hybrid plus/minus eCPs and qCPs). Opposite-charged CPs bond to form DPs. Bonding is via the same method as all eCPs and qCPs use to exert “force, which is by DI-bit information between GPs, which determines SSV, which determines PSR. Bonding is non-displacement (or oscillation within bounds) due to equilibrated SSV/zero-SSV gradient. The DPs densely fill space, being the simplest complex-bond CP. The DPs store energy in the form of displacement and polarization direction. The DPs surround every unpaired CP to form a polarized cloud and contribute to its mass. They serve as the mediators of e and q presence. eDPs are enrolling, making them the simplest complex-bonded CPs, which are supposed to orbit every charged lepton and quark to produce fermionic spin by their Zitterbewegung (ZBW) helical motion. The qDPs form gluon chains between quarks. The hDPs form the tetrahedral, icosahedral, dodecahedral, and fullerene cages surrounding charged leptons (electron, mu, tau) and quarks (up/down, charm/strange, and top/bottom). The CPs within the DPs (eDPs, qDPs, and hDPs) vibrate with ZBW helical oscillations; this ZBW oscillation is the smallest unit of action, h, when at their ground state. The hDPs form hybrid tetrahedra (hybrid tetras) and are the first internal cage of the second and third generation of quarks. hDPs form the body of the bosons: W boson (12 vertex, 6 hDPs, icosahedron, without central unpaired CP). Z boson (20 vertex, 10 hDPs, dodecahedron, without central unpaired CP), and Higgs (8 icosahedra in cubic orientation, 96 vertices, 48 hDPs). The hybrid tetra acts as a NBT (Nuclear Binding Tetra) in several different situations: internal to the baryons (proton: uud quarks and open vertex), and first layer of nuclear structure layering (alpha: NBT bound to 2 protons and 2 neutrons). The NBT/hDP forms naturally in the DP Sea and binds its vertices (2 plus/2 minus) to opposite charge quarks (up/positive and down/negative) to form the baryons (3 quarks and 1 empty NBT vertex). The proton’s NBT has three hDP vertices occupied by uud quarks, leaving one vertex unoccupied with a positive charge. The neutron’s NBT is filled with udd quarks, leaving a negative vertex open. The open vertices serve as primary binding sites for nuclear bonds. Two DP Sea hybrids can bond to form a hybrid Tetra, which is used as an NBT. The NBT can bond its four vertices with an opposite polarity nucleon, such as an alpha particle, which 2 protons (plus vertex onto minus NBT vertex) and 2 neutrons (minus bonded to positive). This is the first layer nucleus and each additional nucleon binds its open NBT vertex onto the opposite charge area on the surface of the alpha.

\item[Golden Ratio (\(\phi\))] \(\phi = (1 + \sqrt{5})/2 \approx 1.618\): The mathematical constant governing 600-cell inflations and symmetries, enabling self-similar layers without perfect commensurability.

\item[Grid Point (GP)] Fixed positional address nodes in the lattice space. The GPs, CPs, and DI bits are conscious points that exist as entities capable of carrying data packets, awareness of their environment, arising from the 120 HCP 600-cell vertices, whose apparent number has vastly inflated to create the framework of space (GPs), the entities occupying space (CPs), and the tokens used to communicate between the entities (DI-bits). GPs provide the 3D metric for space, each with an address established relative to the origin, the Big Bang address (0,0,0). The GPs follow rules of DI-bit communication between GPs, inform the local environment of a CP’s presence, vector sum the DI bits and vector sum their direction and magnitude, and enforce the exclusion rules preventing over-occupancy.

\item[Hypericosahedron Conscious Point (HCP)] One of the 120 vertices of the 600-cell: acts as an integration hub, holographically processing universe state and mediating CPP dynamics without physical movement.

\item[Lattice Physics (LP)] The extension of CPP incorporating the 600-cell as a 4D topological template, providing permissive pathways for displacements and inflations.

\item[Nuclear Binding Tetra (NBT)] A hybrid 4CP tetra (2 qCP/2 eCP) enrolled from the DP sea, serving as a central binding site for nucleons in light nuclei like $^4$He.

\item[Planck Sphere Radius (PSR)] The local “horizon” around a CP where DI-bit interactions remain coherent; emerges from SSV magnitude, with high SSV yielding finer granularity (smaller PSR) and warped effective distances.

\item[Quark Conscious Point (qCP)] A CP with plus/minus polarity, associated with hadronic interactions and strong-like forces in the lattice.

\item[Space Stress Vector (SSV)] The vector at each GP representing the cumulative tension/compression produced by overlapping DI bits. The SSV modulates inflations and PSR, yielding emergent relativistic effects like time dilation. The space containing all SSVs is a vector field, with gradients, divergence, and curls due to the summation of the DI-bits at each GP.

\item[Zitterbewegung (ZBW)] Helical oscillation in DPs around a CP, generating intrinsic spin and magnetic moments; sums across quarks for nucleon spin, interacting with bond fields for spin-orbit coupling. \end{description}
